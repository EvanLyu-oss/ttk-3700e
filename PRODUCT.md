## Product Purpose

TTK-3700E is presented as a silicone-based, PFAS-free polymer processing aid masterbatch in an LDPE carrier. The website helps a visitor understand the product, compare application requirements, inspect measured data and take original technical documents into an internal evaluation. The original request confirms that the site must present this material globally in multiple languages.

## Positioning

The product combines a 5% active-content silicone masterbatch with documented film-property comparisons and three SGS reports covering total fluorine, 671 targeted PFAS analytes and RoHS substances. Its distinction is the combination of formulation, processing guidance and inspectable evidence; no exclusive market, performance or certification claim is established.
